\olsection{Barcan Formulas}

CBF. $\Box\lforall[x][!A(x)]\lif\lforall[x][\Box!A(x)]$

CBF$\Diamond$?. $\Diamond \lexists[x][!A(x)]\if\lexists[x][\Diamond!A(x)]$

BF. $\lforall[x][\Box!A(x)]\lif\Box\lforall[x][!A(x)]$

!!^{derivation} of~$\Proves[\Log{CQML}] \Box\lforall[v][!A] \lif\lforall[v][\Box!A]$ 
(converse Barcan formula).

\begin{prooftree}
\AxiomC{$\Discharge{\Box\lforall[v][!A]}{2}$}
    \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lforall[v][!A]}{1}$}
    \RightLabel{\Elim{\lforall}}
    \UnaryInfC{$\Subst{!A}{c}{v}$}
\DischargeRule{$\Box$\Ax{K}}{1}
\BinaryInfC{$\Box\Subst{!A}{c}{v}$}
\RightLabel{\Intro{\lforall}}
\UnaryInfC{$\lforall[v][\Box!A]$}
\DischargeRule{\Intro{lif}}{2}
\UnaryInfC{$\Box\lforall[v][!A] \lif\lforall[v][\Box!A]$}
\end{prooftree}
